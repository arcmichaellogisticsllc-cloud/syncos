# SyncOS product context

Document version: v1 draft
Last updated: 2026-09-23

Based on the owner's instructions and docs/product/syncos-operating-model.md. This is development context; commercial positioning and claims remain subject to owner review.

## Product and audience
SyncOS coordinates Sync Comm Systems' telecom operations from demand and crew capacity through field evidence, customer acceptance and financial records. Jackson Telcom operations have been absorbed into SyncOS. This is an operating platform for the business; a software-subscription business model has not been established.

Users include internal operations, growth, finance and executive staff; partner company administrators; and foremen from Sync employee and partner crews. Customers and partners need clear scope, responsibilities, readiness requirements and evidence of completed work.

## Work and value
Intended outcomes: identify the next accountable action; prepare crews for assigned work; preserve evidence and correction history; bill customer-accepted quantities; record external payments with proof. These are product goals, not measured customer results.

The website explains and recruits, login authenticates, SyncOS routes users by authorized identity, Sync Admin controls, SyncField executes, and Command Center prioritizes. Company approval, crew readiness and work-order mobilization remain distinct.

## Language and onboarding
Use direct operational wording: work order, assigned crew, missing documents, customer acceptance, accepted quantity, payment proof. State who acts next and what prerequisite is missing. Preserve precise approval states. Avoid generic AI claims, unsupported performance promises, invented testimonials and suggesting a recommendation is an approval.

For a foreman, the first useful outcome is opening authorized assigned work and understanding the prerequisites to record production. For a partner administrator, it is completing required company information and seeing the next review step. For operations, it is identifying readiness blockers and assigning the responsible action. Marketing signup advice must not introduce self-selected privileged roles or bypass review.

## Research and proof gaps
No independently verified customer interviews, conversion benchmarks, pricing, competitor superiority claims or testimonials supplied. Treat such assumptions as unverified. Demo organizations and financial records are test evidence, not customer endorsements or actual revenue.

## Pilot measures
Record task completion, time to first assigned task, unclear steps, validation failures, correction/retry success and access denials in the pilot evidence log. No third-party tracking or marketing campaigns are authorized by this document.
