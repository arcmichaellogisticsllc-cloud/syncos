# Quantity reconciliation walkthrough

Use dedicated synthetic records until supervised pilot acceptance is complete. A quantity review records the reviewer's reconciliation; it is not customer acceptance, a contract amendment or authorization to send money.

1. Sign in with the authorized QC/completeness reviewer account. Open **Customer QC** and select the submitted daily report.
2. Expand the work item in **Reconcile production quantities**. Compare its original quantity, submitted correction history and actual source documents.
3. Choose its **Classification**:
   - **Primary installed work**: one independent work item. Enter the stable work reference used in the source documents. Reuse that work's correction history when its quantity or date changes.
   - **Separately approved additional work**: a genuinely separate authorized scope. Enter its distinct work reference and actual additional-work approval reference. This classification does not independently approve a premium rate.
   - **Included within another work item**: a quantity already contained in one underlying work item. Use **Find underlying work**, select that reviewed item, and confirm the subset does not exceed it. For 886 feet including 180 feet of rock, the 180-foot record is a reference; installed footage remains 886.
   - **Summary of existing work items**: select each distinct underlying item once. The summary must equal their total. A summary of 404 and 144 feet is 548 feet, not another 548 feet of production.
4. Enter the governing source reference and reconciliation findings. Click **Save quantity review**. If confirmation is interrupted, retry with the same entries.
5. Verify the work shows its reviewed classification. Changed quantities, dates, work identity or submitted corrections require renewed review. A changed underlying item also invalidates its dependent reference review.
6. Complete evidence review and record the actual customer decision separately. Summaries and subsets cannot receive independent billable acceptance. An accepted quantity cannot exceed the reconciled effective source quantity.
7. If financial records already exist, use controlled financial adjustments before changing the quantity classification. Never create another work reference merely to bypass an existing claim.

Raw unreviewed entries remain visible for reconciliation. They are not verified installed production. This workflow does not automatically detect every geometric overlap or prove that an external approval is genuine; reviewers must inspect the governing source documents.
