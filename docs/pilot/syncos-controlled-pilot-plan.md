# SyncOS Controlled Pilot Plan

## Scope

- 1 real project.
- 1 work order.
- 1-2 Partner crews.
- 1 Partner Admin.
- 1-2 Foremen.
- Small internal Executive, Operations, and Finance group.
- Live automated Partner payments disabled.

## Day 0

- Create production tenant and reference data.
- Invite Partner Admin.
- Complete company onboarding checklist.
- Verify crew, workers, credentials, headshots, and Foreman.
- Issue agreement/work order artifacts.
- Complete mobilization readiness.
- Verify maps and Today/Field access.

## Day 1

- Foreman opens Today.
- Complete JSA.
- Record daily production.
- Test loss/reconnect while app remains open.
- Submit production once.
- Confirm no rates or finance data shown to Foreman.

## Day 2+

- Record Customer QC.
- If correction required, preserve cycle 1 and resubmit controlled correction.
- Record reinspection and final accepted quantity.
- Generate exports/closeout.

## Finance

- Convert accepted production to billable.
- Issue invoice.
- Record cash.
- Apply customer cash.
- Generate settlement/payable.
- Keep payment execution manual/disabled.

## Intelligence

- Recalculate Partner performance and capacity.
- Run opportunity matching.
- Review Command Center actions.
- Confirm no auto-award, auto-assignment, auto-payment, or auto-lifecycle mutation.

## Staging Acceptance

Run the same flow with synthetic data before controlled pilot.
