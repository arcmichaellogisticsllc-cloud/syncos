# Pilot measurement plan

Use the preserved demonstration workspace to rehearse both Sync and partner crew journeys. Record the release SHA, test actor, selected assignment and scenario for each result. Demo quantities and financial amounts are not operating revenue.

| Decision | Measure | Evidence | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Can a foreman start the right work? | Time from sign-in to identifying assigned work and its blockers | Facilitated observation on the intended device | Fix ambiguous routing or prerequisite messages |
| Can the crew submit reliable evidence? | Successful submissions, failed attempts and retry outcome | Request/record identifiers and audit history, without credentials | Check duplicate prevention and correction guidance |
| Can operations distinguish readiness gates? | Incorrect company/crew/mobilization interpretations | Scenario notes and selected next action | Revise guidance while preserving gates |
| Does acceptance control finance? | Accepted quantity versus billable quantity and recorded payment amount | Customer decision, billable/payable lineage and safe totals | Investigate discrepancies before expansion |
| Are access boundaries effective? | Expected denied actions and unintended visible data | Role-specific negative checks | Treat exposure as a release blocker |
| Can the team recover? | Last successful backup, tested restore and observed recovery time | Backup and restore reports | Assign an operational owner and resolve gaps |

Record actual counts and elapsed times; no invented conversion targets. Separate automated synthetic results from real-device observations. Do not install third-party tracking, send outreach or copy private operational data to analytics services as part of this plan.
