# Field evidence supervised walkthrough

Use dedicated synthetic pilot records. Do not change actual contractual requirements merely to make a test pass.

1. An authorized operations/project/safety manager opens **Work safety**, selects the assigned work-order version and enters **Customer evidence requirements**. Record the governing document/reference, required categories/counts and whether capture times are mandatory. Click **Approve evidence requirements**.
2. The assigned Sync or partner foreman completes that day's approved safety prerequisites and opens the daily production report.
3. In **Photos and evidence**, choose a supported original up to 20 MiB. Select its category, verify the suggested device capture time, enter capture location and describe what it shows. Click **Upload evidence**.
4. If disconnected, verify the device shows a waiting upload. Reconnect and click **Retry upload**. Confirm one server record and download the original. Do not discard your source files.
5. Confirm the screen identifies missing required categories. Submit the day only when required uploads are present. Submission and readability/customer acceptance are distinct states.
6. An authorized QC reviewer opens **Customer QC**, selects the report and downloads originals. Record **Readable** or **Unreadable**, findings and the verification checkbox; click **Save evidence review**. Unreadable evidence requires a replacement, not alteration of the original.
7. Confirm completeness only after required readable files are available. Open the customer inspection cycle with the real customer source reference.
8. Select the production record and documented customer outcome. For acceptance, enter the customer's quantity and select the readable evidence covered by that documented decision. Click **Record customer decision**.
9. Check the retained report revision, evidence original and decision history. Internal completeness/QC alone must not produce customer acceptance or a payable.

Repeat with both workforce types. Physical-phone tests must separately exercise connection loss during upload, resend after timeout, browser/app suspension and work shutdown before queued production replays. Record device/OS, actions, observed outcome and evidence; these steps have not yet been physically witnessed.
