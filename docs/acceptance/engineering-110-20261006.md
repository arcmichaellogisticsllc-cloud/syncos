# Engineering continuation: account programs and interface controls

The operational pilot remains blocked. This is a local schema-110 continuation of candidate 47b480c, not a declaration of application-wide completion or a deployed release.

## Implemented

- First-class account programs tied to an existing account onboarding profile.
- Preserved original documents (PDF/JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MiB), checksum verification, duplicate-upload protection, tenant-wide read/update authorization and download audit records. Originals are stored in PostgreSQL, so their recovery is part of database recovery.
- Immutable governing policy revisions, effective dates, required documents and source originals. Approval explicitly confirms the source. Superseded policies and previous reviews remain accessible as history.
- Submitted/approved/rejected evidence reviews with reasons, expiry and stale-review protection. A policy revision requires fresh review. Expired policies do not silently reactivate an earlier policy.
- Calculated document readiness. Configured programs block onboarding advancement to approved, market-assigned or mobilized when current requirements are unresolved. No contract, work authorization, customer acceptance or financial record is created by this readiness check.
- Onboarding board pagination, stable ordering, invalid-page rejection, contact/account consistency, inactive-owner rejection and prevention of moving a profile to another account.
- Readable account names for onboarding readers without permission to open the separate organization workspace.
- Bounded authorization lookup with a fail-closed timeout and retry; existing same-identity offline field behavior remains separate.
- Actual destructive-action separation in invoice and candidate detail, and additional matching action groups across operational, financial and intelligence workspaces. Existing permission checks, disabled conditions and confirmation dialogs are retained.
- Corrected visible sprint-era messages that called implemented settlement, billing-package and external-payment recording workflows unavailable. Actual integration limitations are preserved.
- A reusable accessible boundary covers 29 existing dialogs, providing an accessible name, keyboard containment, Escape through the existing dismiss action, and return-to-trigger focus. It retains the existing dialog content and workflow callbacks.
- Payment and export batch directories now use validated paging; older batch items are loaded with bounded concurrency. Removed the 25-batch cutoff from payment/export attention views and payment-item reconciliation choices. Failed loads remain visible; unauthorized item views are hidden.
- Added click-by-click program document instructions to the identity and delivery training guide.
