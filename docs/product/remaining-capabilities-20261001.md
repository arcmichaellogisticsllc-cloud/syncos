# Wider SyncOS product backlog

Latest status: `docs/acceptance/product-workspaces-20261004.md` and `docs/product/deferred-work-20261004.md` supersede the remaining-work status below. This document preserves the earlier checkpoint.
This backlog remains separate from controlled crew-pilot acceptance. It does not narrow the operating scope: both Sync employee and partner crews, safety, field evidence, customer acceptance, customer receivables and partner payables remain required. Password recovery, intake, templates and inventory are product work still to complete; SSO and magic links are future enhancements.

| Capability | Engineering work | Completion evidence | Pilot relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-service password recovery | Recovery request and trusted email delivery; expiring one-use tokens stored as hashes; rate limits and non-enumerating responses; password replacement, session revocation and audit; accessible request/reset screens. | Expired/reused token rejection, account enumeration resistance, delivery failure handling, session invalidation and successful user recovery. | A controlled pilot can use an owned administrative recovery procedure; no claim that self-service exists. |
| Customer/service inquiry intake | Public or approved customer intake; validation, consent, attachment restrictions, deduplication, assignment and acknowledgment; linkage into existing opportunity/project workflows; anti-abuse controls. | New, duplicate, incomplete and malicious requests; owner notification and tracked follow-up; inquiry cannot create unauthorized work or approve commercial terms. | Not required if pilot work is provisioned through reviewed internal intake. Partner recruitment intake is a distinct workflow. |
| Configurable form templates | Versioned template administration with field types, required rules, conditional sections and approved publication; record snapshots preserve the version used; rendering, export and permission rules. | Published-version stability, old-record readability, validation parity across UI/API and draft/offline handling. | Existing fixed forms may serve approved pilot requirements. Template configuration must never remove safety, acceptance or financial controls. |
| Full material/reel inventory reconciliation | Receiving and supplier references; serialized reels/lots; warehouse/crew transfers; issue/return, installed use, offcuts, scrap and adjustments; counts, custody, units and project reconciliation. | Beginning balance + receipts/transfers in − issues/transfers out ± approved adjustments = ending balance; concurrent transfer protection; duplicate/retry tests; reel length reconciliation and audit. | Scope the pilot explicitly if physical stock is tracked externally. Existing field material entries do not prove a complete inventory ledger. |
| SSO — future | Approved identity provider and tenant/domain policy, user lifecycle, session handling, recovery and secure account linking. | Provider acceptance, tenant isolation, deprovisioning and fallback recovery. | Future enhancement, not a current pilot prerequisite. |
| Magic-link login — future | Trusted email delivery, one-use short-lived links, replay/rate-limit protections, session handling and device behavior. | Expiry/replay tests, safe redirects, account linking and device/browser acceptance. | Future enhancement, not a current pilot prerequisite. |

Prioritize actual pilot blockers first. These entries are not promises of implemented screens. Track implementation and acceptance separately; future authentication options do not substitute for secure recovery of current password accounts.
